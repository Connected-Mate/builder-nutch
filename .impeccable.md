## Design Context

### Users
People who build and ship production software with several paid Claude Code and Codex accounts. Their job is to know which account can work, connect another account through the official browser flow, and launch work without hunting for login screens.

### Brand Personality
Direct, capable, focused. The user's brief is explicitly for builders who ship. Confidence comes from accurate account states and actions that do what their labels say.

### Aesthetic Direction
Build on Codenotch, preserving its native macOS notch, rings, positioning, and small-footprint behavior. The app and website share white/light gray surfaces, charcoal controls, Bricolage Grotesque and real service marks. The notch alone keeps its compact dark surface, with neutral white/gray indicators. The user explicitly rejected generic blue Forms and neon green branding. No purple gradients, generic card grids, fake testimonials, or unsupported unlimited-usage claims.

### Existing Design Tokens
Extracted from Sources/DesignSystem and Sources/Notch. Palette: black notch/card; ring track #303030; bar track #2D2D2D; ample #00FF88; watch #F2FF00; critical #FF3F00; primary white; secondary #808080. Usage counts: notch 3, card 2, ring track 3, ample 3, watch 3, critical 2, primary 9, secondary 7. SwiftUI system typography; notch percent 14.22pt semibold, title 13.69pt semibold, body 9.48pt regular. Ring 44pt. Settings spacing 6/8/10pt. These are historical upstream values, superseded by the neutral palette below. Retain notch geometry and spacing, with Bricolage typography and grayscale indicators.

### Design Principles
1. Keep Codenotch recognisable; extend the product rather than replacing its useful interface.
2. Show the account, connection state, remaining quota, and action in one readable row.
3. Unknown usage is unknown, never zero. Explain when a first session is needed.
4. Browser authentication stays with the official tools. No password or token fields.
5. Changing the selected account affects new sessions; never silently terminate running work.
6. Support keyboard navigation, VoiceOver, reduced motion, and resizing.

## Builder Nutch brand
User-selected name: Builder Nutch. Ambitious, direct, resourceful. Audience owns multiple AI subscriptions for experiments, R&D and production. Promise: put existing subscriptions to work, spend attention on building, and choose an eligible account at the next launch. No historic lowest-price claim or guarantee of uninterrupted running conversations. Preserve account identity and storage on rebrand.

## Assistant-first UX (6 September 2026)
Global Add assistant → provider catalog → official sign-in → optional nickname and emoji. Show actual account profiles rather than a fixed two-provider sidebar. Separate browser accounts expose website access without invented usage. Invisible Auto-hide reveals from the selected screen edge while the app keeps running.

Interactive website illustrations use clearly labeled sample data; no invented testimonials or account access.
